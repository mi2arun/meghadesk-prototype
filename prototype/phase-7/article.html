How refunds work and typical timeframes

When you request a refund, here's what happens behind the scenes — and how long it usually takes to reach your account.

Updated 12 days ago · 5 min read

How refunds work

When you ask for a refund, the money doesn't move instantly — it travels through a few different systems before reaching your account. Here's the typical flow:

  1. We confirm your refund request and process it on our side.
  2. The refund is sent to your payment provider (the company that processed your card when you paid).
  3. Your payment provider hands it to your bank.
  4. Your bank approves it — this is usually the longest step.
  5. The funds appear on your statement.

Most refunds complete cleanly in a few business days. The exact timeline depends on your card type and where your bank is — see the next section.

How long does it take?

Timeframes vary by card type. Here's what to expect on average:

  • Visa or Mastercard — 5 to 10 business days
  • American Express — 3 to 5 business days
  • Debit cards — same-day to 3 business days
  • International cards — up to 14 business days
  • Bank transfer — 2 to 4 business days

If you don't see your refund within these windows, please contact us and we'll trace it on our side.

Why might my refund be delayed?

Most delays come down to one of three causes. None of them require action on your part — but knowing what's happening helps.

Bank delays

Your bank's batch processor occasionally returns a refund — usually a short-lived issue on their side. When this happens, our payment provider automatically retries on the next batch, typically within 24 hours. You don't need to do anything; the refund will land once it goes through.

Your card was canceled

If the card you originally paid with has been canceled or closed, the refund will sit in escrow with the card issuer. In this case you'll need to contact your bank directly — we're not able to redirect a refund to a different card.

Currency conversion

If your card uses a different currency than the original charge, exchange-rate movement can leave a small balance discrepancy. This is rare and usually self-resolves. If a few days pass without a fix, our team can adjust the amount manually — just reach out.

How to check your refund status

You can see exactly where a refund is from your account:

  1. Sign in to your Acme Cosmetics account.
  2. Open Orders and find the original purchase.
  3. Look for the refund status under the order — it'll read Issued, Processing, or Completed.

If the status hasn't moved in a few days past the typical timeframe for your card, that's the right moment to get in touch.

Still waiting?

If your refund hasn't arrived in the expected window, please contact us. We'll trace it on our side and let you know exactly what's happening — usually within an hour during business days.
